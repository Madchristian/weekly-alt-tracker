# WeeklyAltTracker 2026.10.7-2

## Icons on the Currencies page

All eleven currencies now show their Blizzard icon before the balance and beside the name in the tooltip. Icons come directly from the WoW client and use a consistent size of 12 × 12 pixels. If an icon is unavailable, the text remains readable.
