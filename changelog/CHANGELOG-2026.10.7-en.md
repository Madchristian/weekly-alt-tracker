# WeeklyAltTracker 2026.10.7

## New Currencies page

The new **Currencies** page shows eleven Midnight currencies per character at a glance. First come **Nebulous Voidcores**, which you spend on bonus rolls and to pick gear at the Voidforge.

It also lists Shards of Dundun, Restored Coffer Keys and Coffer Key Shards, Untainted Mana-Crystals, Venomblight Manaflux, Tidal Spark Dust and the warband currencies Voidlight Marl, Undercoins, Corrosive Coins and Coiled Filament.

A row tooltip lists every balance with this week's progress and marks account-wide or warband-transferable currencies. Balances survive the weekly reset.

Shards of Dundun moved from Crest Sources to the new page; your existing Dundun balances are kept. Navigation now has twelve sections.
